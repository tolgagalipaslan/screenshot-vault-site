A useful place for
what you capture.

Bring capture, organization and text recognition into one Windows utility. Keep your screenshot folders and choose how much background indexing you need.

01 / CAPTURE

Only what you choose.

Use a configurable global shortcut to select a frozen screen region. Captures happen on your request, not continuously in the background.

02 / BROWSE

Your library, in place.

View your screenshots in the Gallery and add existing folders without copying or moving their files. Search recognized text with local OCR indexing.

03 / TRANSLATE

Words within reach.

Keep the captured image, OCR text and translation in a compact floating widget. Always-on-top is available and can be changed in Advanced Settings.

Local by design

Offline words.
Clear boundaries.

The four included translation directions run on your computer. No cloud translation account or model download is needed for these routes. Models load when required.

Tesseract OCR includes English, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. Windows OCR uses recognizers installed on your system. The application interface is in English.

How your information is handled ↗
  • English → Turkish
  • Turkish → English
  • Korean → English
  • English → Korean

Other translation directions are not included. OCR and translation results can be inaccurate; always check important text.

Set it up your way

Ready when you are.

Choose your screenshot folder, shortcuts and theme in Basic Settings. Pause background OCR when you need to. Advanced Settings keep the technical options out of the way.

Optional Windows startup opens Screenshot Vault in the system tray. Closing the main window leaves it available; tray Exit stops it. Startup never takes a screenshot for you.

Still a beta.
Always worth checking.

Screenshot Vault is experimental software. Features can fail, OCR and translations may be wrong, and bugs may affect local data. Back up important images and check results. Support and fixes are not guaranteed; applicable legal rights remain unaffected.
